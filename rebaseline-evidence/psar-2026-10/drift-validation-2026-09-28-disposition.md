# Disposition of the drift-regime validation (matrix `drift-validation-2026-09-28`)

**What was checked.** The drift-regime estimator that the low-power frequency protocol uses (1e-5 to 1e-3 MW) was compared, on an immutable 66-cell matrix at commit `38c8c95` (2026-09-28 to 2026-09-30), against (a) a forced full-discard reference estimate and (b) a doubled drift window, at the boundary, two interior and the top frequency points of each power and core: 24 points, 1R and 9R. Tolerances: |dG/G| <= 0.5 %, |dphi| <= 0.5 deg (`drift_validation_report_strict.json`, `helpers/paper-rerun/compare_drift_validation.py`).

**What the report says.** 19 points pass, 5 are inconclusive, 0 fail, 0 missing; overall status `inconclusive` (the report does not pass). The five inconclusive points are the 1e-5 MW boundary and interior points (1R: boundary, interior1, interior2; 9R: boundary, interior1). At each of them the drift-versus-full comparison is inconclusive because its full-discard reference estimate did not converge (`both_converged: false`): the unconverged side is the reference, not the drift estimate. The window-doubling comparison passes at all 24 points (worst |dG/G| 0.17 %, worst |dphi| 0.073 deg). A separate lock-in re-estimate on the full-discard records (`drift_lockin_ref_output.txt`) agrees with the drift estimates within 0.07 % in gain and 0.08 deg in phase at the inconclusive points.

**Disposition (project, 2026-10-05; owner to confirm).** The drift-regime estimates stand as the published low-power values, with the window-doubling result and the lock-in re-estimate as their independent support. The five 1e-5 MW drift-versus-full comparisons are recorded as inconclusive comparisons whose reference did not converge, not as failures of the estimator and not as a pass; no point is excluded. This record supports the protocol of campaign `psar-2026-10-01` (same estimator and settle rule) and was produced at commit `38c8c95`; it is not a rerun of that campaign, and the campaign's 24 frequency verification reports are its acceptance evidence.
